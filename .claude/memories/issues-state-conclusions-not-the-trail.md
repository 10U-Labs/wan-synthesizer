# An issue states the conclusion, not the trail

When a later finding settles a question an issue was weighing, the body is rewritten to state the answer and what it rests on. The options it ruled out and the reasoning that ranked them are removed, not kept beside it.

**Why:** once a finding settles the question, the options it ruled out are the trail of getting there, not findings. Kept beside the answer, they are noise a reader has to get through to find it, and an option left standing reads as still open. The rule comes from `deltahdl` and was adopted here on 2026-10-07 under issue #255.

**How to apply:** after adding a finding, re-read the whole issue and cut every sentence the finding makes moot. A person's decision is written in the same way, per [a-decision-rewrites-the-issue](a-decision-rewrites-the-issue.md).
