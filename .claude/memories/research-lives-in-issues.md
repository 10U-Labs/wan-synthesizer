# Research lives in issues

When a session plans work that spans more than the session, such as a survey of
every tenant's published syntheses or a comparison against the sibling
repositories, the plan and each finding go into GitHub issues as they arise, one
problem per issue, with enough of the method in the body that a fresh session
can run it from the issue alone.

**Why:** a session can be lost, and its task list and scratchpad with it;
research recorded only there is lost too. The rule comes from `deltahdl` and was
adopted here on 2026-10-07 under issue #255.

**How to apply:** this is the one kind of finding that is filed rather than
solved. A problem met while working is solved, per
[what-does-not-get-filed](what-does-not-get-filed.md); a plan of discovery, and
each mismatch it records, is filed at once. Put the command or query that
reproduces a finding inline in its issue, so it does not depend on the
scratchpad. The grain is
[an-issue-is-split-by-problem-not-by-fix](an-issue-is-split-by-problem-not-by-fix.md).
