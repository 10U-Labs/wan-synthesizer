## Overview

A defect noticed while doing something else is filed as an issue before the reply is written. Do not end a reply with "one more thing" or "two other things I noticed". A finding parked in a chat reply is lost the moment the session ends: nothing links it to the work, nobody who was not in the conversation can pick it up, and the user is left holding a decision they have to remember to act on.

## Conventions

### File it in the shape an issue takes

Six sections for the program, two for everything else, and then say in one line which number it is. The reply still names the finding; the difference is that it points at something a reader can open.

### Three cases are not this

Something inside the scope of the task in hand is done, not filed. A finding that reaches a genuine fork is asked about before filing. And a finding that cannot carry a "Problem" section saying what the defect costs is dropped, not mentioned: offering it to the user as a question files it in their head instead of in the repository.
